# Narration: Studiohakim, 18 Years of Income

Documentary pace, about 140 words a minute. Each line plays over its scene. Scenes stretch to fit the read, with a short breath before each cut.

| Scene | Line |
|---|---|
| Title | Eighteen years. One studio. This is the story of Studiohakim's income. |
| 01 The starting years | It began modestly. For three seasons, sales held between twenty-three and twenty-six thousand dollars a year. |
| 02 The first climb | Then came the first climb. By the 2012 season, sales reached forty-seven thousand dollars, the high point of the early years. |
| 03 The long slide | But the peak didn't hold. After a long plateau, income slid for years, bottoming out below twenty thousand in 2019. A fall of nearly sixty percent. |
| 04 Turbulence | The recovery was turbulent. Sales climbed past fifty-eight thousand by 2022, then dropped by almost a third the following year. |
| 05 The step change | And then, 2024 changed everything. Sales more than doubled, to ninety-two thousand dollars: the best year on record. |
| 06 Settling at $80K+ | Since then, the business has found its footing. Eighty-three thousand in 2025. Over eighty-eight thousand in 2026, with weeks still to go. A new floor, at eighty thousand dollars. |
| Four eras | Across four eras, the story is clear. Today, Studiohakim earns nearly two and a half times what it did just a few years ago. |
| Outro | From twenty-three thousand, to a steady eighty thousand and beyond. Studiohakim. |
