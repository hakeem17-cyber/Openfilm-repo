# Meme sound effects 2026

Sixty sound effects from one file uploaded by the owner.

- **Individual files:** `sound-fx/meme-2026/`, one `.m4a` per effect, numbered in the order they appear.
- **The whole file:** `assets/meme-sound-effects-2026.m4a` in both `car-talk` and `podcars-clips`. The `#t=` column plays one effect straight from it.

## How the names were made

The file was split automatically at its silent gaps, then each piece was run through speech recognition. Nobody has listened to them.

- **clear** (17): a spoken phrase the recogniser was confident about.
- **unsure** (19): a short word, shout or laugh that may be misheard.
- **named by owner**: labelled by the owner after listening.
- **no clear words** (20): music, booms, whooshes or noises. These are named `NN-sound.m4a` and need a listen to label.

A piece may hold two effects run together, or half of one, where the gaps were unusual.

## Before publishing

- The source was labelled "no copyright". That label has not been verified, and several of these are well-known clips from films, games and shows.
- Effects 8, 28 and 52 contain language that may not suit every audience.
